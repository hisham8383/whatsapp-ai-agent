Privacy policy

{{ b.name_en }} · WhatsApp assistant · Last updated {{ updated.isoformat() }}

{{ b.name_en }} uses an automated assistant on WhatsApp to answer questions and to book, move and cancel appointments. This page explains what it stores and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and the name on your WhatsApp profile, or the name you give us.
  • The messages you send us and our replies.
  • Your appointments (service, date, time, staff).

We never ask for health details, ID numbers or card details. Please don't send them.

Why

Only to answer your questions, manage your appointments, and let our staff continue the conversation when you ask for a person.

Who processes it for us

  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries the messages.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that writes the assistant's replies. Messages are sent to it only to produce a reply.
  • Google Cloud hosts the service and its database.
    • {% if calendar %}
  • Google Calendar holds the appointments.
    • {% endif %}

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Message text is deleted automatically after {{ retention_days }} days. Appointment records are kept as business records.

Deleting your data

Send us a WhatsApp message saying "Delete my data" and ask for a person. Our team will delete your messages, name and number from our systems within 30 days and confirm on WhatsApp. Appointment records we must keep for our business are anonymized instead.

لماذا

فقط للإجابة عن أسئلتك وإدارة مواعيدك وتمكين فريقنا من متابعة المحادثة عندما تطلب التحدث مع موظف.
